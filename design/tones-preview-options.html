AFloating previewRecommended

  • Point at a tone: a card shows under that row with what you said over what that tone types, the changes in colour. A little notch points at the tone.
  • Slide along the tones and only the text changes. Slide down the column into the next place and the card follows you.
  • Move off the tones and it goes, about a sixth of a second later. Click a tone to use it.
Good: nothing in the list ever moves, and it closes the moment you leave.
Cost: while it shows, it covers the rows under it. A row near the bottom of the window gets its card above instead.

BClick to open

  • Click a row (or its chevron) to open it; opening one closes the other. Personal starts open here.
  • In an open row, pointing at a tone swaps the preview to it, and moving off the tones goes back to the one in use.
  • Pointing at a closed row's tones only highlights them. Clicking one picks it and opens the row.
Good: calm and predictable: nothing opens or closes unless you click.
Cost: a click before you see anything; when the row above closes, the one you clicked slides up.

CAlways open

  • Every place always shows what its tone in use types.
  • Pointing at another tone swaps that place's preview in place; moving off goes back.
  • No opening or closing at all.
Good: the simplest. Everything is on the page, and nothing moves.
Cost: the page is about twice as long, and Auto cleanup goes below the fold. It is the "a lot to read" the redesign moved away from.

Whichever you pick

  • The colours always show. With Auto cleanup on None, no plan or tones off, the preview used to show your words unchanged with no colour. That is what the dev app showed, since its cleanup is on None. Now it shows what the tone would type, labelled Would type, with a line saying why it's paused (try the State switch).
  • Which tone is which. The one in use keeps its raised chip, the one you point at gets a soft fill, and the preview says In use or Click to use.
  • Steady height. A preview is as tall as that place's longest tone, so moving between tones never changes its height.
  • Pointing works with SayControl in the background, as in a browser. Today the tones only react while SayControl is the active app, which is likely why they sometimes seemed to stop working.
  • Auto cleanup and Your rules stay as they are. Rules stay one text box for now; separate lines are a later change.
