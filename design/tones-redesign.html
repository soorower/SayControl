v2: what gets built

  • Direction A, trimmed. Title, one line under it, then the four places as rows, then Auto cleanup and Your rules in one box. No hero banner, no section labels, no hints.
  • A closed row is 68 px: 14 px top and bottom, 18 px left, 16 px right. A 32 px icon tile (radius 9), 13 px gap, the name (display face 16/20, weight 500) over one short line (12.5/17, muted, one line). Then up to four 18 px app icons (radius 5, 5 px overlap, a tooltip naming every app) and the tone switch.
  • The tone switch: a trough with 3 px padding and radius 10. Buttons are 28 px tall, 13 px side padding, 12.5 px weight 500, radius 7. The picked one sits on a raised thumb that slides 300 ms on cubic-bezier(.3,1.18,.45,1). A pointed-at one gets the hover fill.
  • Opening: moving the pointer onto a row, or focusing one of its buttons, opens it straight away. Only rows below it close; rows above stay open, so the row under the pointer never moves. The height grows over 320 ms on cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). The content fades in over 220 ms after 60 ms and rises 4 px. A row opens on pointer movement, not on enter.
  • Closing: 250 ms after the pointer leaves the whole places box, every open row folds over 240 ms, and the content fades out over 160 ms. Moving between buttons or rows never closes the row under the pointer.
  • An open row adds the preview, inset 63 px left (lined up with the name), 16 px right, 14 px bottom. First a line with the tone's name (600, ink) and its short line (12.5/17, muted). Then 8 px down, the well: 12/14 px padding, radius 10, a 58 px label column, 7/12 px gaps. You say is 13/19.5 muted, with the cut words struck. It types is 14/21 ink, with the changes washed. The highlights are on whenever the row is open.
  • Which tone it previews: the button under the pointer. Off the buttons, an open row keeps showing the last tone pointed at, and a row that just opened shows the picked one. Switching tones changes the text at once, and the height grows or shrinks downward over 320 ms. A click picks, and the row stays open.
  • Cleanup None and No plan: both lines show exactly what was heard, with no highlights, and the switches fade to 55%. Tones off: the rows fade to 45% under the existing alert. Auto cleanup changes only on a click. Pointing at a stop scales its knob 1.12× and darkens its name; pointing at the well washes its changes in. The well’s min-height is its tallest level (None), so nothing jumps. Your rules are as picked.
