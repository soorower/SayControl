How it works

v2.1: what gets built

  • Each tab is a dial. The knob turns through six points (eight on Controls), every 3.8 s, with a soft tick. The wheel, a drag, a click or the arrow keys turn it, and pointing at a demo holds it.
  • One point open at a time: a title of 2–4 words, one line, and a few seconds of motion. See more under Controls and Command mode, and All terminal commands under In a terminal, open the rest in place while the dial waits.
  • Sound is muted until the first click, then the speaker in the header turns it off. With reduced motion, nothing turns and each demo shows its last frame.
