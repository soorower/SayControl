v2: what gets built

  • Plan is direction B: your plan as a big card at the top, then Free against Pro as one table. In dark mode the card has only the glow, without the grid behind it.
  • Free vs Pro differ by more than the word count. Command mode, spoken controls (screenshots, files, links, terminal) and Ask ChatGPT/Claude/Grok/Perplexity are crossed out on Free.
  • After the 2,000 words (try “Free, words used up”): the plan card says so when it happens. The compare table doesn’t mention the extra takes.
  • No Models section in the user build. The developers’ build keeps Model exactly as it is.
  • The rail’s foot is no longer your name and photo. It says whether SayControl is ready to dictate and which version you’re on, with Check for updates.
  • General has real Mac keycaps, glyph and word on each key, every key lined up on the right. Appearance gains Pill when idle: always shown, or hidden after 5 s and back when you dictate or point at it.
  • Motion: the sheet rises in and drops away; the highlight slides between sections; pages come in block by block; numbers count up.
  • Same sizes as the app: 980 × 690 sheet, 224 rail, built from Theme.swift’s colours, so the Swift version can match it closely.
