What this page does

  • Two jobs, kept apart. Transcription (your voice to text) and Polish (tidying the text, and command mode) each have their own section, source and model.
  • Transcription runs on SayControl Cloud, on this Mac, or on your own key. Polish runs on the cloud or your own key; a model on this Mac comes later.
  • The route at the top: Talk → Transcription → Polish → Pasted, and one line on where your audio and your text go. Anything not set up is outlined. Click a step to jump to it.
  • Less text: what each section does sits behind the ⓘ next to its title and shows on hover.
  • This Mac: a Live switch (on by default). On, the models that keep up live on this Mac come first; the rest, and Whisper always, write when you let go. Which ones keep up is measured on the Mac itself: switch “This Mac” in the bar to see an M1 with 8 GB, where only Parakeet stays live. Each family has its sizes; download, use or delete right in the row.
  • API keys: pick a provider, paste a key, and the models that key can use appear. A key saved once works for both jobs: an OpenAI key added for transcription is already there under Polish.
  • Polish has a Try it with the model you picked, so you see what it does to a messy sentence and how fast it answers before you dictate with it.
  • No agent voice here. It is a SayControl Cloud feature and stays in Agents, signed in.
  • Same parts as Settings today: the sheet, rail, rows, pills and switches from settings-redesign.html, so the Swift build reuses them.
